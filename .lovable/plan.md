# Homepage conversion polish

## Goal
Make the homepage conversion-test ready without changing its established visual structure.

## Changes
- Give the homepage one primary offer: **Get My Free Website Design** in the header, mobile navigation, sticky mobile button, and opening area.
- Keep Google as the lower-friction secondary offer farther down the page: **See How My Business Ranks on Google**, with **Get My Free Google Report** reserved for the short report form.
- Make the short form respond to the offer it displays, so website-design submissions and Google-report submissions receive matching confirmation and booking language.
- Separate the one-time **Free Google & Local Visibility Snapshot** from the paid **Monthly Google & Local Visibility Report**, clearly stating when each is delivered.
- Keep **Live Within 14 Days** as the commitment and label the faster result as an average, removing any apparent 4-day/14-day contradiction.
- Replace selective-review wording with honest, policy-safe language that asks every completed-job customer for an honest review.
- Replace guaranteed ranking language with accurate local-visibility language.
- Add a premium tilted report preview showing a visibility score, map ranking, competitor reviews, website performance, calls, bookings, and recommended next steps.
- Reduce repetition by tightening overlapping copy and removing only the most redundant offer explanations; retain the long-form sales structure and all major proof sections.

## Technical details
- Reuse the existing design tokens, buttons, reveal motion, forms, and responsive patterns.
- Build the report preview as a responsive interface mockup, not a decorative stock image.
- Preserve the dedicated Google report page and its Google-first conversion path.
- Verify the homepage at desktop and the current tablet-sized preview, including form labels, CTA hierarchy, and report readability.
